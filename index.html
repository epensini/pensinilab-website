How we work

Mechanisms first. Applications follow.

We combine spectroscopy, scattering, microscopy, interfacial characterization, thermodynamic analysis and molecular modelling, choosing the methods that best test each physical question.

Contaminant separation, solute movement through aquifers and mechanoelectric materials are important systems in which we investigate transferable physical principles—not boundaries on the questions we ask.
